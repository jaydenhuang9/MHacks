⋆˚꩜｡.ᐟ our mission

Waste systems shouldn't wait to fail. Flux gives campuses a living model of their infrastructure, so every truck, every bin and every mile is a decision made before the overflow, not after it.

Act early

Forecast fill levels six hours ahead and respond while there is still time to choose the gentlest option.

Waste less effort

Send a truck only when simulation says it matters. Fewer miles means fewer emissions.

Keep people in charge

Flux recommends. A human operator approves, modifies or rejects every plan.

─ °❀⋆ the problem

Fixed routes meet real life.

Collection schedules ignore game days, move-out weekends and festivals. Bins overflow, trucks rush out after the fact, and every extra mile adds fuel, noise and emissions. Flux replaces "react and dispatch" with "predict, test and adapt".

⋆˚꩜｡.ᐟ how it works

Four steps, one loop.

  • 01 · PREDICT

    See it coming

    Telemetry and event context become six-hour fill forecasts for about 36 virtual smart bins.
  • 02 · SIMULATE

    Test every response

    Five interventions are simulated side by side and scored on overflow prevented, collection effort and environmental impact.
  • 03 · OPTIMIZE

    Route only what's needed

    When collection is the right call, ReRoute plans the truck's path through the selected stops.
  • 04 · ADAPT

    A human decides

    The operator approves, modifies or rejects. Approval deploys the plan and updates the twin.
