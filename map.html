How optimization works

The system estimates fuel using truck MPG and route distance. It compares a simple baseline route with an optimized route. The optimizer can:
  • Use the closest available truck
  • Combine nearby pickups
  • Avoid unnecessary miles
  • Reduce estimated fuel use
Fuel savings are simulated estimates for this hackathon demo.
